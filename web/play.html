Lane ribbon

The three pips are the three lanes. Red means a boulder has claimed that lane and is grinding — get out of it.

The whip

Tap or press space. It only reaches your own lane, 2–5m ahead. Crack a spider at 3m for a perfect and a speed burst.

Damage
